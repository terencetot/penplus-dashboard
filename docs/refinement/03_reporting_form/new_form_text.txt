PEN-Plus project country reporting form
NCD, PEN-Plus Second Phase
Purpose
The NCD National Professional Officer in each WHO Country Office completes this form with the national programme and the PEN-Plus sites. The returns from all countries are aggregated at regional level to follow progress and to produce the regional reports.
How to complete
1.	One return per country per quarter. Report the quarter named in section 0 and no other.
2.	Write 0 where there were none. Leave a cell blank only if the figure cannot be produced; a blank is read as not reported, never as zero.
3.	Choose from the drop-down lists rather than typing. The permitted answers are printed in italics under each heading.
4.	Every Yes on a policy or plan needs the title of the document that proves it.
5.	Do not add, delete or move rows, tables or sections. The return is read automatically.
